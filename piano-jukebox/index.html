Connecting the piano

  1. Use Chrome or Edge on the computer paired with the piano (Web MIDI).
  2. Turn off Bluetooth on your phone, then pair the WIDI Jack in Windows Bluetooth settings.
  3. Allow MIDI access when the browser asks. The piano appears under the device button.
